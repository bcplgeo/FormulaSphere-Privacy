FormulaSphere Privacy Policy

How FormulaSphere handles information when you use the application.

Effective date: 18 October 2026

FormulaSphere is an offline-first formula, calculation, constants and unit-reference application developed by MannzVerse. This policy explains information the app stores, information that may be sent to external services, and choices available to users.

1. Information FormulaSphere collects

FormulaSphere does not require an account. Its core calculator functionality does not ask you to provide your name, phone number, contacts, precise location, microphone recordings, camera images, SMS, call logs, photos or personal files.

The app may store information locally on your device, including:

  • Favorite formula identifiers.
  • Calculation history, including the formula used, entered values and calculated result.
  • Display and calculation preferences, such as theme, precision and angle-unit preference.
  • Cached reference information for selected periodic-table entries.

2. How local information is used

Locally stored information supports favorites, calculation history, preferences and faster reference access. FormulaSphere does not intentionally transmit locally stored favorites or calculation history to advertisers.

3. Information sent to external services

The core formula database and calculator logic are bundled with the app. Some optional or network-enabled features may contact external services:

  • Periodic-table reference lookup: opening an element reference may request public reference information from PeriodicBubbles. The request may include the selected element's public slug; FormulaSphere does not intentionally send calculation history or favorites to that service.
  • Reference links: selected entries may open Wikipedia or other source websites.
  • Google Play: the rate-app action opens the Google Play listing.
  • Email: feedback opens the installed email application addressed to mannzverse.apps@gmail.com. An email is sent only if you choose to send it.
  • Sharing: app and formula sharing use the device's sharing mechanism when available.
  • Fonts: the web shell may request Spectral and IBM Plex font resources from Google Fonts when network access is available.
  • Advertising: the app integrates Google AdMob for banner advertising, as described below.

4. Advertising, AdMob and consent

FormulaSphere integrates Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. Development builds may use Google's test advertisements. A production release may use the developer's configured AdMob app and ad-unit identifiers.

When advertising services operate, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as IP address, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostic information, approximate location inferred from network information, and advertising-related data. The exact information processed can depend on the device, Google Play services, region, consent choices, and advertising configuration. FormulaSphere does not intentionally send locally stored favorites or calculation history to AdMob.

FormulaSphere requests consent information through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before requesting ads and is designed to request ads only when the SDK reports that ads may be requested. Consent forms and privacy options vary by region and applicable requirements. The availability of a consent form does not mean that every user in every region will see the same form.

Google processes information under its own policies and service terms. Review Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob Help for more information.

5. Health and nutrition information

FormulaSphere includes informational health, nutrition and medical-reference content. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. Content is for education and reference and does not replace professional medical advice. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for diagnosis, treatment or other medical decisions.

6. Data sharing and sale

MannzVerse does not sell personal information. FormulaSphere does not intentionally share locally stored favorites, history or preferences with advertisers or data brokers. External services may receive information when you use a feature that contacts or opens that service, and advertising providers may process information as described in Section 4.

7. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored favorites, history, preferences and cached reference data remain on your device until you remove them through available app controls, clear the app's storage, uninstall the app, or reset its data. FormulaSphere does not maintain a server-side user account containing these local records.

8. Security

Locally stored information is protected primarily by the device's operating-system and application-storage security. No method can guarantee absolute security. Keep your operating system and Google Play services updated and protect your device with an appropriate screen lock.

9. Children's privacy

FormulaSphere does not require account registration and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising treatment and age-related settings must be configured appropriately by the developer in AdMob and Google Play Console. Parents or guardians should supervise use of health and educational content where appropriate.

10. Your choices

  • Use the app without creating an account.
  • Clear calculation history using the app's available library controls.
  • Remove favorites individually.
  • Avoid optional external reference lookups, sharing, rating and feedback actions.
  • Use privacy or advertising controls presented by Google where available.
  • Uninstall the app or clear its storage to remove locally stored application data.

11. Privacy inquiries

For privacy questions, contact mannzverse.apps@gmail.com.

12. Policy changes

This policy may be updated when FormulaSphere's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page indicates when this version took effect.
